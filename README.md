# Revenue Leakage & Billing Analysis

A Python-based finance analytics project that identifies revenue leakage, outstanding payments, refunds, discounts, and collection risks from invoice-level billing data.

## Business Objective
- Measure billed vs collected revenue.
- Identify outstanding and high-leakage invoices.
- Find high-risk customers, regions, services, channels, and payment methods.
- Analyze discount and refund exposure.
- Provide actionable finance/collection insights.

## Tools
Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Jupyter Notebook

## Workflow
1. Data Understanding
2. Data Cleaning
3. Exploratory Data Analysis
4. Advanced Business Analysis
5. Final Business Insights

## Leakage Risk
- Low Risk: <10%
- Medium Risk: 10%–30%
- High Risk: 30%–50%
- Critical Risk: >50%
